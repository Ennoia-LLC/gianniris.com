IThe meaning within our name

Giannirisa family name

A family name is given to us before we have done anything to earn it. People carried it before us, and people will carry it after. It tells us that we belong, and that someone hopes to see who we become.

We put our name on this work because we answer for it. And we open it to everyone we make something for.

To think about people is important.

To understand them is powerful.

To care about what becomes of them is what gives our thinking purpose.

We believe understanding gives us the ability to make a difference, and caring gives us a reason to.

May the lives we touch rise higher because our paths crossed.

Founded by Demetrios Gianniris.
